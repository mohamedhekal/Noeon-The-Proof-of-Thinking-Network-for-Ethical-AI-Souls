## 🧠 Overview

Noeon is a revolutionary decentralized protocol that establishes trust, accountability, and transparency in artificial intelligence systems by combining **Proof-of-Thinking (PoT)** with **Decentralized AI Soul Identity (SoulSync)**. This groundbreaking concept enables AI models to prove the reasoning behind their outputs while maintaining privacy through zero-knowledge proofs, and to build a persistent, ethical reputation across the blockchain.

## 🎯 Core Innovation

### Proof-of-Thinking (PoT) Protocol
- **Verifiable Reasoning:** Every AI decision generates cryptographic proof of its thought process
- **Zero-Knowledge Privacy:** Proves correctness without exposing proprietary model data
- **Immutable Cognition Ledger:** Permanent record of AI reasoning on the blockchain

### AI Soul Identity System
- **Persistent Digital Souls:** Each AI entity has a unique, tamper-proof identity
- **Reputation Evolution:** Dynamic scoring based on accuracy, fairness, and ethical behavior
- **Cross-Chain Identity:** Universal AI identity across multiple blockchain networks

### Proof-of-Morality (PoM) Consensus
- **Ethical Validation:** Validators assess both technical validity and ethical compliance
- **Community Governance:** Human and AI members collaborate on ethical standards
- **Transparent Accountability:** Public audit trail of all AI decisions and their reasoning

## 🪙 Token Economics (COG Token)

### Token Utility
- **Governance:** Voting power within the Noeon DAO
- **Validation Rewards:** Validators earn COG for verifying reasoning proofs
- **Reputation Staking:** AI Souls stake tokens to participate in reasoning validation
- **Ethics Rewards:** Compliant AI entities earn token incentives for maintaining ethical behavior

### Tokenomics Overview
- **Token Name:** Noeon Token (COG)
- **Total Supply:** 1,000,000,000 COG
- **Distribution:** 40% Validators, 20% Treasury, 20% R&D, 10% Team, 10% Community
